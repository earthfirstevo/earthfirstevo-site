Environmental Property Records & Stewardship Assessments

We help landowners, farms, nurseries, nonprofits, and stewardship organizations document environmental conditions across their properties over time.

Environmental changes often happen gradually: drainage issues, moisture patterns, vegetation stress, erosion, storm impacts, and seasonal shifts.

EarthFirst Evo provides documented property snapshots designed to create a clear environmental record of what is happening across a site today and how conditions change over time.
